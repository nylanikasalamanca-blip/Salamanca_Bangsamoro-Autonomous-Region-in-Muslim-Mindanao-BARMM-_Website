Popular Destinations

Explore the stunning natural wonders and sacred landmarks of Bangsamoro

1. Asik-Asik Falls

Alamada, North Cotabato / Maguindanao Border

Asik-Asik Falls is famous for its unique curtain of water cascading down a wall of lush green foliage. Unlike traditional waterfalls, water emerges directly from spring outlets in the mountain cliff face.

2. Masjid Dimaukom (Pink Mosque)

Datu Odin Sinsuat, Maguindanao

Painted bright pink to symbolize peace, love, and unity, this iconic place of worship attracts visitors from around the world. It stands as a striking symbol of contemporary Bangsamoro faith and hospitality.

3. Panampangan Island

Sapa-Sapa, Tawi-Tawi

Panampangan Island boasts the longest sandbar in the Philippines, stretching across clear turquoise waters. Its untouched beaches offer peace, coconut palm fringes, and unmatched tropical scenery.

4. Sheikh Karimul Makhdum Mosque

Simunul, Tawi-Tawi

Established in 1380 AD, this is the oldest recorded mosque in the Philippines. It features original wooden pillars that have endured for over six centuries, marking the birthplace of Islam in the archipelago.

5. Lake Lanao

Lanao del Sur

Lake Lanao is the largest lake in Mindanao and the ancestral heartland of the Maranao people ("People of the Lake"). Surrounded by cool mountain air, it is a center for trade, culture, and traditional crafts.
