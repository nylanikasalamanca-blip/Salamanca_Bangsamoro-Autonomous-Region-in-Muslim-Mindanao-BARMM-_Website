Travel Tips

  • Dress modestly when visiting religious sites and mosques (cover shoulders and knees).
  • Always bring cash (PHP) as ATMs can be limited in island provinces like Tawi-Tawi.
  • Coordinate with local tourism officers for island hopping tours and local guides.
  • Respect local Islamic customs and prayer times.

Packing Checklist

  • Modest clothing (scarves, long trousers, sleeved shirts)
  • Light breathable cotton clothes for tropical weather
  • Sun protection (sunscreen, hat, sunglasses)
  • Dry bag & water shoes for island trips
  • Rechargeable power bank & cash currency
