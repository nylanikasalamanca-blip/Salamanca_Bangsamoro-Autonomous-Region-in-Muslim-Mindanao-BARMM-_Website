About the Region

The Bangsamoro Autonomous Region in Muslim Mindanao (BARMM) is a unique destination in the southern Philippines rich in Islamic heritage, vibrant royal cultures, and serene natural beauty. Comprising Basilan, Lanao del Sur, Maguindanao del Norte, Maguindanao del Sur, Sulu, and Tawi-Tawi, BARMM offers travellers an unforgettable journey through pristine white-sand islands, dramatic waterfalls, historic grand mosques, and intricate artisan traditions.
